# Benchmarks

Charts on this page are drawn from the result files in
[`bench/results`](https://github.com/neonjava/Atlas/tree/main/bench/results)
when the site is built. Each dot is the **median** (middle value) of at least
five runs; the box covers the middle half of the runs and the thin line goes
from the slowest to the fastest run. A small box means the runs agreed.
